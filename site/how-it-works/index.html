A DELIBERATE, USER-INITIATED WORKFLOW

How it
works.

Cookie Share Next acts only when you ask it to save or switch an account. Encryption happens before synchronization, and decryption stays in your browser.

01

Sign in to the service

Choose an OAuth provider configured by the server, or use a backup login key that you previously generated. Authentication opens access to your encrypted buckets; it does not reveal their contents.

02

Unlock your vault

Enter the vault password locally in the extension. The password derives the encryption keys in your browser and is never sent to the service.

03

Save an account

On the active website, choose “Save current account.” The extension captures the permitted cookies and same-origin local/session storage needed for that website's login state.

04

Encrypt before upload

The extension encrypts the account-state payload and its private label. Only the encrypted envelope, an opaque ID, size, and timestamps reach the server.

05

Switch when needed

Choose a saved account for the current site. The extension replaces that site's cookies and local browser state, then reloads the active tab so the selected session can take effect.

06

Stay in control

Rename, export, or delete individual entries from Settings. You can also delete all encrypted buckets, reset a backup key, sign out, or use your own compatible server.
